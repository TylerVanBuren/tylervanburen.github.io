Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 1st 2026

YourCore is designed to respect user privacy. The app does not require users to create an account, provide a name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or other personal information.

YourCore does not directly collect, store, sell, or share personal user data for its own databases or user profiles. However, the app may use third-party services, such as advertising providers, that may collect limited information as described below.

Information We Collect

YourCore does not directly collect personal information from users. The app does not currently operate user accounts, cloud profiles, server-side quiz history, or user-submitted personal data.

Local App Data

YourCore may store app-related information locally on your device. This may include quiz progress, results, favorites, preferences, or similar app functionality.

This local information stays on your device and is not transmitted to YourCore-controlled servers.

You can remove locally stored app data by clearing the app’s data through your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

Advertising

YourCore may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services, such as Google AdMob. These services may collect or receive information from your device, such as device identifiers, IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and advertising-related data.

This information may be used by advertising providers to display ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain security, and improve advertising services.

YourCore does not directly sell personal information. Third-party advertising providers process data according to their own privacy policies, terms, and user settings.

Analytics

YourCore does not currently collect analytics data for its own internal databases.

In the future, YourCore may add internal analytics to help improve the app, improve the matching algorithm, understand app performance, or identify areas that need improvement. If analytics are added, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain what data is collected, how it is used, and what choices users have.

Current analytics status: YourCore does not currently use internal analytics to store user quiz results or behavior on YourCore-owned servers.

Data Sharing

YourCore does not directly sell, rent, or trade personal user data.

Data may be processed by third-party services used in the app, such as advertising providers. These services are responsible for their own data practices.

Data Security

Because YourCore does not currently collect or store personal user data on YourCore-controlled servers, YourCore does not maintain personal user profiles or cloud-based quiz histories.

Information stored locally on your device is subject to your device’s own security settings and protections.

Children’s Privacy

YourCore does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If future features change how data is collected or processed, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes may be made to reflect updates to the app, new features, advertising practices, analytics, legal requirements, or other operational changes.

If YourCore begins collecting analytics or other data in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe those practices.
